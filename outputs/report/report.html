How to read the metrics

  • Satisfaction: utility of each completed visit (interest match x quality), reduced by up to half when the place is over capacity, minus 0.06 per minute queueing and 0.01 per minute travelling.
  • Overcrowded visits: visits made while arrivals exceeded the place's capacity in that 15-minute slot.
  • Peak load: highest arrivals / capacity at any attraction in any slot. Load Gini: inequality of attractions' average load 10:00-17:00 (lower is more even).
  • Feasible: no failed stop (closed, sold out, too late), back before the end of the day, within budget and walking limit.
  • CO2 counts taxi and transit legs per passenger; walking is zero.
